Pro tips & shortcuts

  • Click any TxRef or phone number to copy it instantly — paste it straight into your Telebirr / CBE app to double-check.
  • Press 1–5 to jump between tabs and / to focus search. ? shows all shortcuts.
  • Export CSV from Payments or Students for backups, Excel reports and accounting.
  • The browser tab title shows (N) pending receipts — pin the tab and you'll never miss one.
  • Working late? Use the speaker button to mute the chime — Telegram alerts still come through.
  • On Chrome mobile: menu → Add to Home screen for one-tap access to this portal.
  • Always open View Proof and compare the amount + TxRef with your bank SMS before approving.
